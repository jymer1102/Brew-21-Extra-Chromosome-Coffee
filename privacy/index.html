Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Brew21 Extra Chromosome Coffee Co. (“we”, “us”) runs this website and a coffee bar at 209 W Commercial St, Springfield, MO 65803. This policy explains what information is handled when you visit brew21.coffee.

What we collect

This website does not have accounts, forms, or advertising trackers, and we do not run analytics. If you email us, we receive your email address and message and use them only to reply.

Online store

Shopping, checkout, customer accounts, order emails, and payments happen at store.brew21.coffee and are provided by Square. Square collects and processes the information you enter there under its own privacy policy and sets its own cookies. We see the order details needed to fulfill your order.

Third-party services

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which means your IP address is sent to Google. Links to Google Maps, Instagram, and Facebook take you to those services, which have their own policies. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitor IP addresses for security and operations.

Cookies and local storage

We store only your cookie choice in your browser’s local storage so we do not ask repeatedly. You can change it any time with “Cookie settings” in the footer. Optional cookies, if we ever add any, will stay off until you accept.

Your choices

You can ask us to access or delete anything you have emailed us. We do not sell personal information. The site is not directed to children under 13.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will change the date above. Questions: extrachromosomecoffee@gmail.com.
