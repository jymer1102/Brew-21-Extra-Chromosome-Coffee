Luke 1:37: Nothing is impossible with God

We’re the Justis family

Chris, Whitney, and Wyatt. Wyatt was born with an extra copy of the 21st chromosome, which means he has Down syndrome. He’s smart, kind, funny, and always the life of the party.

Since he was little, we’ve prayed and dreamed of starting a business he could be part of, one that would let us work together. So the vision of a mobile coffee bar was born. God is so good and always has the perfect plan.

Our mission is to serve great coffee and to give back to the special needs community. Because of you, our business is growing and our dreams are too. We’re now opening a brick-and-mortar shop inside the Upside Down Mercantile, and we’re so excited to see where the Lord takes us.

Thank you for letting us live out our dream, and for letting Wyatt’s light shine.
